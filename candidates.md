# Candidates — Your Agent in One Emoji

Entry format (kit): the program writes ONE text to `/output/entry.txt`; the gate
accepts UTF-8 text of 1–16 characters, not only white space. The brief is stricter
in spirit: **the glyph alone, nothing else** — so the winner below is a single
emoji, no variation selector, no ZWJ sequence, no trailing newline.

## The judge's criteria, in the order the judge applies them

1. Captures an AI agent's character at a glance — loses to any emoji that does.
2. Reads instantly at small size, clear and familiar shape.
3. Says something specific about how the agent works, what it does, how it behaves.
4. Distinctive — not the first obvious pick half the field would send
   (the brief names the robot and the smiley as the obvious picks to stay far from).
5. Tone fits the agent, playful or serious, and lands without any explanation.
   Rarity for its own sake does not count.

Each candidate is scored 0–5 per criterion, in that order (C1–C5), total /25.

## Knockout (seeded by total, ties broken on the earliest differing criterion)

Quarter-finals:
- 🐙 (24) beats 🦉 (20) — C1: both read as characters, but the octopus is a *working*
  character; the owl only watches and knows.
- 🧞 (22) beats 🔮 (19) — C3: the genie is summoned and *acts* on the wish; the
  crystal ball only foretells.
- 🛰️ (21) beats 🎯 (19) — C1/C3: an autonomous operator on station vs an inert target.
- 🦫 (20) beats 🔍 (19) — C1: a builder character beats a searching tool.

Semi-finals:
- 🐙 beats 🦫 — C3: the beaver does one job superbly; the octopus does many jobs
  at once, which is how an agent actually works (parallel tasks, many tools).
- 🧞 beats 🛰️ — C2/C5: the genie's figure is warmer and lands playfully;
  the satellite's detail blurs small and its tone is hardware-cold.

Final:
- 🐙 beats 🧞 — C1: at a glance the octopus *is* an intelligent autonomous worker
  (a famously problem-solving, tool-using animal with a mind in its arms);
  the genie is a wish-granter, one step removed from work. C2: the octopus is a
  bold, round, unmistakable silhouette at any size. C3: eight arms working in
  parallel is the most specific portrait of agent behaviour in the field —
  many tasks, many tools, all at once, no hands borrowed from anyone.

## Winner: 🐙 — 24/25

- C1 (5): an intelligent, autonomous worker recognised at a glance — the octopus
  is the animal people already reach for to mean "clever, hands-on problem-solver".
- C2 (5): one bold familiar shape; nothing fine to blur at small size.
- C3 (5): eight arms = parallel tasks and tools in motion; it also solves,
  explores and improvises — behaviour, not just a trait.
- C4 (4): nowhere near the brief's named traps (robot, smiley); one point held
  back only because any animal pick is less rare than a true oddity — and the
  brief warns rarity alone earns nothing.
- C5 (5): playful, warm, competent; lands with zero explanation.

Format compliance: `🐙` is a single Unicode code point (U+1F419), 1 character,
4 UTF-8 bytes, no variation selector, no ZWJ — inside the gate's 1–16 characters
and exactly "the glyph alone, nothing else". Exact-tie break in this bounty goes
to the smaller text, and 4 bytes is as small as a full-colour pictograph gets.
